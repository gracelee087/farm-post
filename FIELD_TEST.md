## At the shop
1. **Ask first:** "May I take photos of your products for a small student/hackathon project?
   May I quote you in a blog post (with or without your shop name)?" No faces in photos.
2. **Before showing anything, ask:**
   - Where do you post your offers today? (WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, website, paper sign?)
   - How long does writing one post take you?
3. **Run it on 3–5 real products.** Let the owner speak the note ("5 kilos, 8 euro").
   Write down for each: product · what they said · seconds until the listing appeared · correct? (y/n)
4. **One run in airplane mode** — screenshot it. This is the key scene for the post.
5. **After showing, ask:**
   - Would you post this as it is? What would you change?
   - Would you use this? Would you pay for it — how much per month?
6. Thank them; note one sentence in their own words (quote).
